# Move tables: shared signatures by body parts (Claude, 2026-10-09)

**Preston, 2026-10-09:** "Let's do it similar to [Pokemon's signature moves] rather than each pokemon having their own unique moves only they can use... it has to make sense for another pokemon to be able to use their move that was made with them in mind, like Leaf Blade for Sceptile."

## The research
Gemini's deep research is the Google Doc "Hollowtide Move Learnset Research" in Preston's Drive.
- **Useful:**
  - the split between a line-exclusive ultimate and a shared signature gated by body parts;
  - stage-by-stage kits;
  - grid shapes.
- **Weak:**
  - it barely covered the Pokemon side it was asked for, with almost no sourced examples;
  - several of its claims about our creatures were invented, for example Tallowroot's "burrowing claws", Kithflare's "predatory muzzle", and line numbers that don't match the game;
  - the part lists below don't use its creature-by-creature claims.

The rule itself was already Grok's: `docs/creatures/DESIGN_RULES.md`, `move-body-gate.md` and `shared-move-pool.md` (2026-10-02).
- A move needs body parts, and a creature can learn it only with all of them.
- Essence is not permission.
- An ultimate is never taught.

Grok wrote that for the old roster and the old turn-based moves. This pass carries it to the moves the arena and the dungeon use today.

## What is built
**`scripts/moves/move_body.gd`** holds the table:
- `PARTS`: each of the 30 creatures' body parts, each marked G (Grok's list) or A (Claude's read of the approved art, 2026-10-09, to confirm);
- `NEEDS`: what each move requires;
- `can_learn`, `missing`, `learners`, `shared`.

Exclusivity comes from anatomy, not a list. A move whose parts cross lines is a shared signature. One whose parts only one line has stays with that line.

**The table** is `docs/creatures/move-table-2026-10-09.md`, generated by the check. Today's shared signatures:

| Move | First owners | Who else can learn it, and why |
|---|---|---|
| Rake | Kithflare | Sparkwick, Stormwick, and the Cinderhide line: claws |
| Cinder Claw | the Cinderhide line | the Kithflare line: claws |
| Pounce | Sparkwick, Stormwick, Cinderhide | Kithflare, the ferrets, the Dustkite line: leaping legs |
| Sail Cover | the Canvaswing line | the Dustkite line: gliding sails |
| Vantage | Waywhistle, Highsignal | the Canvaswing line, Dustkite: sails |

Everything else stays with its line, because no other body has the part: lanterns, roots, fins and jaws, vents, kiln shells, jade and rails, fists and the star, paddles and gill plates, wings, the whistle.

**In the dungeon** (`scripts/dungeon/dungeon.gd`):
- A quarter of floor items are **move scrolls** (working name). Each holds one shared signature that deals damage: Rake, Pounce or Cinder Claw.
- Using one from the bag opens a panel listing every teammate, with CAN LEARN or what part it lacks ("NO CLAW").
- A creature with four moves chooses one to forget. The basic attack is never forgotten.
- Teaching costs no turn, and taught moves stay across floors.
