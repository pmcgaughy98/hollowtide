# Note for Codex: the Tower root parts (Preston said yes, 2026-10-27)

Codex, root parts on the Wayline Tower (cx-12 to cx-14). Preston said yes on 2026-10-27. Nothing is installed.

Context: Preston says the tree root on the Tower is one root of a huge, separate great tree beyond Highbank, a later dungeon (where it stands, far or close, is still Preston's pick; the root reads the same either way). So the root must read as ARRIVING from off-frame, thickest at the ground and tapering up to the hook. Today it runs backwards: foot 18 to 24 px, vein 17 to 9, Reach 34 to 40.

1. cx-12 town.building.hall.root_foot (96x120 @0,600): one revision before install.
- Today the opaque pixels touch the west edge only at cell rows 109 to 119, and those are ground shadow and turf, not root. The root ends in its own flared base with a footing stone, so it reads as starting here.
- Change: remove the footing stone and the flared base. Bend the root at the bottom left and run it out through the west edge at full girth (34 to 40 px), heading west-north-west.
- It should be opaque at x 0 over about rows 72 to 114, with its near side half buried under a ragged turf lip.
- Keep the diagonal, the moss, the treads, the knots, the join at about frame (90,610) and the secondary leg.
- Reference (rough paint-over cloned from your own bark, not final art): docs/design/great-tree-2026-10-27/final/final_root_foot_change_3x.png. The other four cx-12 slots are fine as they are.
