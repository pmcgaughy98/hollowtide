# Fifth Audit: Dates, Staleness and Contradictions

Run 24 September 2026 in a claude.ai cloud session, against copies of all 15 uploaded files: the
five documents, the Two-Star page, the context file, the through-line, the three raw chat dumps,
the generated-docs source, the MEMORY index, the Hub's `09_Geopolitics_Sports_Leisure.md`,
`00_WHERE_THIS_LIVES.md`, `Research_Synthesis.md` and the core context file.

**What this audit was for.** The files were written on different days (8, 16, 19, 22, 23 and 24
September, plus chats going back to May), so an older file can state as current something a newer
file, or the world, has since overtaken. The job was to find those, not to re-argue the documents.

**Method.** First, a cross-file pass: every dated or time-sensitive claim compared across files,
newest wins. Second, about 25 web checks on everything that could have moved since each file was
written. Third, the session's own additions from today were audited the same way, because the last
two audits showed a correction is a new claim and needs its own check.

**Limits.** This session's network blocked most news sites, so checks rest on search-result
excerpts, not full articles. Items that rest on one weak source are labeled. This is the same
reasoning checking itself; an independent verifier was not run (see the end).

## 4. Re-checked and confirmed

- Alberta: "roughly two thirds remain" holds. Polls range from 61% (Angus Reid, 61 to 33) to 74% (Research Co., August). Another poll had 64 to 24.
- Unifor and Stellantis: the contract expired 20 Sept with no deal. Talks hit an impasse on 11 Sept over Brampton, and a strike is at least weeks away because conciliation comes first.
- Greenland: signed 22 Sept (a Tuesday), ratification pending, no vote dates announced. New: Frederiksen said on 23 Sept that the agreement depends on NATO membership.
- India 18% since 2 Feb 2026, the 25% oil penalty rescinded.
- Every NATO ally at 2% or more in 2025.
- Euro area growth: the corpus uses the IMF July 2026 figure of 0.9%. An older January figure of 1.3% also circulates. Keep 0.9%, which is the newer one.
- 155mm: 36,000 a month in March 2026 (IG, Stars and Stripes, Military Times, Washington Times).
- PAC-3: Lockheed delivered 620 in 2025; the framework goal is 2,000 a year by 2030.
- Canada in SAFE: agreement concluded in February; formal procurement participation from June 2026.
