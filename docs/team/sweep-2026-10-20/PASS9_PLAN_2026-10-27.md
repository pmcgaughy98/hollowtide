Goal: an alpha nobody can tell was made with AI (same as pass 8). Inputs: the leftovers harvest (H9-01 to H9-58) and the pass 9 live audit (A9-01 to A9-15, screens in `pass9-audit/`). Pass 8 rules stay:

- Checks run as `python tools/debug/run_checks.py <names> --fixed-fps 30 --jobs 1`, never without `--fixed-fps 30`.
- Every Godot run uses a scratch APPDATA. Hash Preston's `team.save`, `team.save.bak` and `menu_preferences.cfg` before and after, and report the hashes.
