## Rules for every lane

- Edit only your own files. No git add, commit, push, stash, checkout or reset. No paid generation. No em-dashes.
- Grok's numbers, Codex's title files and Route 1 stay untouched.
- No new player-facing words. Use existing sounds, icons and tracks.
- Gate: `run_checks.py <words> --jobs 1 --logs <temp>` plus a parse check of each touched script. ui_fit may keep only the 3 known red title points.
- The timing check is load-sensitive. If it fails, rerun it alone once; a repeat failure counts.
