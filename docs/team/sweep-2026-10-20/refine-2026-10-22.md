## Fixed (Claude's areas)
- **Save could be lost to a crash mid-write.** Opening the save for writing emptied it at once; a cut-short or bad file
  then read as "no save", Continue vanished, and the next save wrote over it. Now: write `.tmp`, keep the last save as
  `.bak`, rename into place; load falls back to the `.bak`; a length check rejects bad files quietly. A migrate hook
  is ready for the first save-version bump. `save_check` step 7 proves it.
- **Turns finished behind the pause menu.** Nine timers in the dungeon and every boss-scene pause kept running while
  paused, so a hit landed, cooldowns ticked or a wild creature moved behind the menu. They now pause with the game.
- **Choosing an item from the Bag mid-turn closed the menu and used nothing.** Fixed in the pause menu.
- **Missing art showed nothing.** A recruited Skimlet, Ozbender or Valvekin had an empty portrait box (now shows its
  dungeon still), and a recruit with no small art was invisible on the walk into The Hollow and could break the dawn
  scene (both now fall back to the dungeon art).
- **Dungeon sounds were read from disk on every play.** Now loaded once and reused.
- **Arena checks:** nine were out of date (Ember Feint replaced Flashstep on 2026-10-08, renamed functions, screenshots
  headless). All fixed; the arena behaviour itself was fine. `run_stop_check` no longer times wall-clock milliseconds.
- **Runner:** reports the real cause when a check's screenshot stops it, not the add-on's start-up line.
