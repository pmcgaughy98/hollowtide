## What a later terrain pass should show

Make height readable before multiplying buildings: a loess-colored bluff face/retained terrace at the upper descent; a lower silt landing with exposed roots, sparse reeds and damp timber; and, in a cross-river sightline or later far-bank slice, a broad grassy levee with sloped sides and a crest track set back from the water. Preserve negative space and readable approaches. Real levee form and native vegetation should support the existing material palette and three-quarter game projection.

The new map references establish physical layout and shape. They do not establish the cause of any historic flood, and no current dialogue or quest assigns such a cause. Existing research corrections remain in the field notes; do not average them together with older report claims.
