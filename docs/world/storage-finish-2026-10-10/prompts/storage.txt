Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: one transparent pixel-art building sprite for Hollowtide, a creature-led Mystery Dungeon style game.
Primary request: an original enterable town STORAGE building matching the already installed cozy ochre stone / thick twisted timber-root / moss green cottage architecture. This is a storehouse, with solid wooden wall panels, cross-braced storage shutters and a couple of wooden crates tucked against its RIGHT side. Broad low moss roof, thick root beams, small warm amber windows, broken ochre foundation stones. It must feel like the same world as the existing moss-roofed Home and Item Shop, with a distinct practical storehouse silhouette.
Composition: straight-on game overworld building facade, slight view of the roof surface, absolutely no isometric side wall or camera perspective. Entire building fits inside a 6:5 rectangle, meant to become exactly 144 by 120 game pixels. Bottom foundation at the bottom of that rectangle, highest roof at top. The entrance is offset slightly LEFT of centre: doorway centre at 42 percent of total building width (60 of 144 pixels), reaching the bottom foundation. One single 24-pixel-wide entrance tile, NOT double doors. Keep the lower doorway dark and clear for a separate animated wooden door later. Its cream/ochre framed arch must remain uninterrupted, full coherent drawing. A warm lantern beside it. Keep the wall immediately right of the door clear for a small crate pictogram sign that will be added by the game. No baked text/sign lettering.
Style: crisp detailed pixel-art sprite, limited harmonious ochre / brown / moss / olive palette, top-left lighting. Distinct near-black one-native-pixel outlines around the WHOLE silhouette AND all internal architectural details: window frames/mullions, timber ribs, individual stone blocks, roof sections, shutter beams and crates. Structural contours must pop clearly. Readable deliberate pixel clusters rather than noisy speckles. No blurry painted edges, no photographic realism, no smooth 3D, no airbrush.
Background: actual transparent alpha, no ground tile, no grass rectangle, no cast shadow outside the sprite, no scenery, no UI, no people or creatures, no other buildings. Thin transparent safety padding so nothing is clipped. No checkerboard pattern, no caption, no logo or watermark. Only one complete building. Preserve the top-left light and warm doorway/window contrast.
