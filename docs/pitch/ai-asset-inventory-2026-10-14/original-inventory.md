## Scope and categories

This inventories player-facing content wired into the current pilot and its retained modes/fallbacks. It is **not an exported-build manifest**: no release export or Steam depot was inspected, and no `export_presets.cfg` was present. F5 currently starts `scenes/dungeon.tscn`; the campaign title starts at `scenes/creature_opening.tscn`. Repository presence alone does not establish that an asset is reachable or will ship.

- **AI-generated:** a generative model produced the image, animation, music, sound or prose. Cropping, palette locking, outlining, mixing or retuning afterward retain this classification.
- **AI-assisted and edited:** Claude/Codex authored pixel grids, drawing functions, synthesis recipes or text templates, with directed revisions. These have no image/audio-model output in the stated recipe. They are still AI-assisted player-facing work. Here, “edited” does **not** assert human pixel painting or a human rewrite of every line.
- **Hand-made / third-party authored:** externally authored conventional assets with a recorded author/license, without an AI-generation step in the project's provenance. This is not a claim that Preston drew or composed them.
- **Unknown:** a source-to-file or tool record is missing. Keep this explicit until verified or the asset is excluded from the release.

The [group register](ai-asset-inventory-2026-10-11/asset-groups.csv) covers every media/resource folder found under `assets`, including raw takes and backups. The [reference census](ai-asset-inventory-2026-10-11/source-references.json) records literal `res://` references in scripts, scenes, resources and dialogue. Dynamic paths and optional fallbacks require the consumers below; the census is not a reachability proof. [Method and limitations](ai-asset-inventory-2026-10-11/README.md).

## Pitch and store disclosure draft

> This prototype uses AI-generated creature and environment art, animation, portraits, music, sound effects and narrative drafts. Art was made with Retro Diffusion, earlier Opal workflows and built-in image-generation tools; music and sound use Suno and ElevenLabs. Outputs were selected, cropped, recoloured, outlined, mixed and integrated under the designer's direction. UI, some scenery/effects and synthesized sounds were created through AI-assisted code and revised for the game. Narrative was drafted with AI assistance and remains under editorial review. The current first-party gameplay uses prepared assets and stored text, with no live generative-AI service found in the reviewed gameplay sources.

This describes **pre-generated** player-consumed content, including AI-assisted content. Steam's current survey distinguishes development efficiency tools from content players consume, and separately asks about live generation. Use the final build and marketing assets to complete the actual survey; this draft is not a submitted store disclosure. [Valve Content Survey documentation, checked October 11, 2026](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/contentsurvey).

Static review found no HTTP/LLM generation call in first-party `scripts/`, `scenes/`, `resources/` or `dialogue/`. Conventional dungeon generation, opponent AI, procedural painting and template substitution do not call a generative model. Generation tools in `tools/` run during development. The Godot AI editor plugin/helper is development tooling; its helper documents that it idles without the debugger in exported releases. This is a source review, not a network trace or exported-package audit.

Before a release: reconcile the export contents and promotional images with this inventory; resolve unknown legacy sources/model records for retained assets; verify the account-plan/output rights for Suno, ElevenLabs, Retro Diffusion and Opal; retain font/third-party notices; confirm any human rewrites and replacements; review the final disclosure against that exact build. The existing pitch kit remains a proposal to seek human collaborators, not a promise that generated assets have already been replaced.
