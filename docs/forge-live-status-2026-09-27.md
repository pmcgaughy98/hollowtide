**Production rule:** Opal outputs are image candidates. An approved front/back is the visual authority. Keep the raw source, inspect at native battle scale, then convert magenta conservatively and import reviewed transparent PNGs to Godot. A flattened candidate is not automatically a cutout rig: hidden joint pixels, exact rest-pose reconstruction, pivots, action timing, and engine hookup remain separate work. COMPLETE mode does not guarantee unchanged kept pixels; compare those manually. Protect the generation quota by reviewing one output at a time.

**Battle integration limit:** The live battle script still treats drawn attack sheet F4 as contact for melee and does not yet provide the general event timeline for ranged, beam, guard, interrupt, or hit body sheets. Claude owns that architecture under [Preston's role order](agent-role-charter-2026-09-27.md); Codex owns exact asset preparation and hookup. Do not link a new ranged/beam body sheet into live battle until its timing is implemented and reviewed.
