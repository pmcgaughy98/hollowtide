Stage III • Essence: Ember

Creature Brief

Evolutionary Path: Stage III. Core Identity: This entity retains the face, signature anatomy, and palette of the previous stage while embodying the new specified silhouette.

Physical Attributes: Long-distance runner with a shielded chest furnace. Features plated haunches, a split signal crest, a low aerodynamic torso, and a balancing tail. Stance is a right-facing guard-run with the head held high.

Materials & Finish: Soot and rust primary coloration accented with iron plates and an active ember core.

"Steady and discerning. Engineered for endurance over explosive force. Avoids mounted armor, lanterns, fire wings, or generic draconic archetypes."
