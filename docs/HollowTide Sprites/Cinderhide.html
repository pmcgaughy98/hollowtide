Cinderhide

Field Guide Entry // Hollowtide Bestiary

Essence: Ember
Status: Registered

Anatomy & Temperament

Anatomy: Compact charcoal reptile with a blunt wedge snout. Features two short horn plates, functional ember vents, and sturdy foreclaws.

Silhouette: Defined by a low braced body and a distinct broken sawtooth spine.

Temperament: Exhibits a proud and wary demeanor. Primarily seen in a three-quarter pose facing right, with foreclaw extended.

Palette: Defined by deep coal and iron gray, accented by restrained ember red and orange highlights.

Pose

Three-quarter Right

Classification

Compact Reptilian
