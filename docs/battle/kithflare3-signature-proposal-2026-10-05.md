# Kithflare III signature: proposal (Claude, 2026-10-05)

> Approved by Preston the same day. First playable version: `docs/battle/stormlantern-2026-10-05/README.md`.

A proposal for Preston and Grok Build. Grok owns move rules and numbers (`docs/creatures/DESIGN_RULES.md`); nothing here is in the game.

## What the body gives us

Kithflare III (Ember/Electric, the Ember starter's final form):
- **Storm lantern tail**: a sealed amber-glass globe in forged ribs, a white-gold ember core, lightning arcs around it. The end of a three-stage tail story (bare ember, caged ember, storm lantern). No other line has it.
- **Lightning-rod crest**: a fan of dark rod spines with arcs between them.
- **Bolt horns** and **glowing zigzag seams** down its body.

By the design rules, an ultimate needs a part only that line has, is its hardest hit, and has 2 uses. The storm lantern and the rod crest qualify.

## Recommended: "Stormlantern" (working name)

1. **Tell**: Kithflare III rears up and lifts the lantern high. The crest rods crackle, the sky darkens, and a lightning bolt strikes down onto the crest. It runs along the glowing seams, down the tail and into the lantern.
2. **Charge**: the amber globe overloads from orange to white-hot. Arcs whip around the forged ribs, and the ribs flare open like a cage being unlocked.
3. **Release**: it snaps its tail forward and throws the lantern's core: a huge sphere of ember wrapped in lightning that crosses the stage.
4. **Impact, in layers**: a fire burst, then a web of lightning spreading across the target, then embers raining down and a crackling afterglow. The lantern closes and dims back to its resting glow.

This meets the signature bar from Kingstone Rail (a clear tell, a huge projectile, a layered impact), and every beat uses a part Kithflare III actually has. It also echoes the courier theme: a signal lantern lit by the storm.

**Essence: open, for Grok.** Electric is the new half of the line and reads as the strike. Ember is the line's home and the core of the lantern. The chart reads the move's essence, so the choice decides who it is strong against.

## Alternatives

- **Rod Crest Discharge**: no projectile. Lightning hits the crest and grounds through its claws, and chain lightning races across the floor to the target. Cheaper to animate, but the lantern sits out its own ultimate.
- **Signal Flare**: it opens the lantern and fires one blinding beam of ember and lightning. Strong, but close to Molten Beam's shape, so it would need a different look to stand apart.

## Production notes (for when it is approved)

Stage painting (`MoveData.signature_stage`), RD VFX sheets (16 frames, about $0.07 each: a lightning strike, the charged lantern, the projectile, the impact), the title card, and the camera cut that Molten Glass and Kingstone Rail already use. Attack frames would need Kithflare III poses (a raised lantern and a forward throw), likely 2 to 4 RD edits.
