## Scope and categories

This inventories player-facing content wired into the current pilot and its retained modes/fallbacks. It is **not an exported-build manifest**: no release export or Steam depot was inspected, and no `export_presets.cfg` was present. The current `project.godot` starts `scenes/creature_opening.tscn` (the campaign title); `scenes/dungeon.tscn` remains the dungeon scene. Repository presence alone does not establish that an asset is reachable or will ship.

- **AI-generated:** a generative model produced the image, animation, music, sound or prose. Cropping, palette locking, outlining, mixing or retuning afterward retain this classification.
- **AI-assisted and edited:** Claude/Codex authored pixel grids, drawing functions, synthesis recipes or text templates, with directed revisions. These have no image/audio-model output in the stated recipe. They are still AI-assisted player-facing work. Here, “edited” does **not** assert human pixel painting or a human rewrite of every line.
- **Hand-made / third-party authored:** externally authored conventional assets with a recorded author/license, without an AI-generation step in the project's provenance. This is not a claim that Preston drew or composed them.
- **Unknown:** a source-to-file or tool record is missing. Keep this explicit until verified or the asset is excluded from the release.

The **October 11 snapshot** [group register](ai-asset-inventory-2026-10-11/asset-groups.csv) covers every media/resource folder found under `assets`, including raw takes and backups. The [reference census](ai-asset-inventory-2026-10-11/source-references.json) records literal `res://` references in scripts, scenes, resources and dialogue. Dynamic paths and optional fallbacks require the consumers below; the census is not a reachability proof. [Method and limitations](ai-asset-inventory-2026-10-11/README.md).

## Pitch and store disclosure draft

> This prototype uses AI-generated creature and environment art, animation, portraits, music, sound effects and narrative drafts. Art was made with Retro Diffusion, earlier Opal workflows and built-in image-generation tools; music and sound use Suno and ElevenLabs. Outputs were selected, cropped, recoloured, outlined, mixed and integrated under the designer's direction.
