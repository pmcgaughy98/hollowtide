# Highbank's townsfolk: proposal (Claude, 2026-10-10)

Preston: "Generate monsters that are going to fill the npc roles and what stage they are of their 3 stage
line. Their line won't be obtainable or meetable so we can just make one stage for now to fill the roles we
need for the story up to evolution." Story: `docs/design/pilot-story-2026-10-10.md`.

Synthesis (structure only): Explorers of Sky staffs its town with creatures whose bodies suit the job
(Kecleon's shop, Kangaskhan's storage, Chatot as the Guildmaster's assistant). Hollowtide's crew are
Missouri river pests, so the townsfolk are Missouri creatures too. Each is one form of an unbuilt
three-stage line; none spawns in a dungeon. **Names, looks and essences are proposals for Preston.**
Stage 3s follow his rule that evolutions read as monsters, not Earth animals.

| Role | Working name | Creature | Stage | Essence | Why it fits |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Guildmaster | Starward | Punchybun line (built) | 3 of 3 | (its line) | Preston's call |
| Guild assistant (greets you, runs dispatch) | Wayling | eastern bluebird (Missouri's state bird) | 1 of 3 | Gale | the eager young messenger at the Guildmaster's side |
| Item Shop | Swaphoard | packrat | 2 of 3 | Loam | real packrats trade: take a thing, leave a thing |
| Storage | Pouchbill | American white pelican | 2 of 3 | Tide | keeps your things in its throat pouch |
| Scroll Keeper | Inkhoot | barred-owl monster | 3 of 3 | Gale/Murk | the night-wise keeper of move scrolls, inked feathers |
| Growth Shrine | Cairnshell | three-toed box-turtle monster (Missouri's state reptile) | 3 of 3 | Stone/Bloom | ancient; its shell has grown into a little shrine of standing stones |
| Training Yard | Crawdukes | crayfish monster | 3 of 3 | Sinew/Tide | boxing-glove claws, an armoured sparring coach |

Art: Retro Diffusion front takes, two each ($0.18 a take), 96 px for stage 1, 112 for stage 2, 128 for
stage 3, Mirekit and Starward as style references; files in
`docs/creatures/arena-starters-2026-10-07/forms/<name>_arena_front_take{1,2}.png`. Preston picks a take
for each; then a turn and a walk if the town scenes need them to move (most stand at their counters).
