# Electric and Ice essences, and the electric ferret (2026-10-01)

## Decision (Preston)
Electric and Ice join the essences. In the game now: both are in `CreatureData.Essence` (added at the end, so saved creatures keep their values), the Hollow rule covers them (Hollow hits them x2 and they hit Hollow x2), and signature cards have colours for them (Electric `#fce45c`, Ice `#bce8fc`). Everything else is neutral until the chart below is approved.

## The electric ferret: a creature for a friend
Preston's friend, who loved Pikachu, passed away. Her twin sister asked Preston to make a creature, an electric ferret. This creature is hers as much as his: her answers shape it before any image is made.

**Starting idea (PROPOSAL, for her to change):** a long, springy ferret that builds static by doing the ferret "war dance" (the happy sideways hop ferrets do), so its fur puffs and crackles the more playful it gets. A bottle-brush tail that sparks at the tip. Its own creature, never a Pikachu copy, but it can carry one quiet nod to it if she wants (a colour, a cheek mark, a sound).

**Questions to ask her:**
1. Her friend's name or nickname, if she'd like it to inspire the creature's name.
2. Her favourite colours.
3. Three words for her personality (playful, fierce, gentle, loud, kind...).
4. Anything she loved besides Pikachu: an animal, a place, a song, a hobby, a saying.
5. One special creature, or a full three-stage line?
6. Should it echo Pikachu at all, and how?

When the answers are in: write the brief, make one front with Retro Diffusion, show it to her, then the 8 directions after she approves.
