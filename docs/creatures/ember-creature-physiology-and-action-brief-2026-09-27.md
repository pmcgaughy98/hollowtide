# Nine Ember forms: appearance, ability, and action direction

**Status: design proposal for Preston's review, 2026-09-27.** This reassesses the generated creatures against their current front art and the actual equipped move resources. The image is evidence for shape, marking, and visible openings; it cannot by itself prove organs, behavior, speed, intelligence, or ecology. Those are clearly marked below as interpretations. The Gemini *Hollowtide Animation System Research* report supplied useful questions, but its statements about physiology, locomotion, scale, and rigging are proposals, not observations or instructions. Preston's latest battle decision is intact-sprite breathing and whole-sprite travel for contact moves.

## What the three lines look capable of

### Cinderhide → Clinkerhide → Ironvent: retain, vent, and direct heat

The approved images show a compact-to-massive charcoal reptile lineage with embedded orange fissures, progressively larger shoulder/forelimb mass, a rising broken ridge, and a more assertive brow/crest. Heat containment and release are a strong reading because the orange appears *inside* dark material and recurs in the same body regions. **Proposed ability:** the hide mineralizes around internal heat channels; growth increases how long each form can hold pressure and how precisely it can release it. The powerful limbs and crest support physical strikes, but the line's core identity is **living heat regulation**, not simply brute strength.

**What remains uncertain:** The orange openings look like heat sites, but whether this is literal lava, mineralized tissue, stored combustion, or another Ember biology has not been established. The image alone does not support precise temperatures or industrial-kiln anatomy. A subtle whole-sprite breath is the current idle; no cutout rig is required to express these abilities.

### Kithflare → Wayburn → Coalcrest: carry and shape a signal

The current approved images show a slender four-legged reptilian line, triangular heads, pale angular throats, long split-tipped tails, and a striking **connected orange zigzag** running through tail, flank, and toward the neck. The limbs and changing posture suggest these creatures can move well. They do **not** prove that locomotion itself is a supernatural power, that they have heat sacs, or that every attack should be a dash. Gemini's relay-runner treatment overreached here. The most distinctive visible feature is the continuous marking.

**Recommended ability proposal:** the zigzag is heat-responsive living tissue used to **carry patterned thermal light along the body**. A pulse can start at the tail, run the flank, and resolve at the pale throat or raised crest. Kithflare can flick short signals; Wayburn can redirect or briefly hold them; Coalcrest can send a larger, sustained or precisely patterned flare. Their build allows them to inhabit long routes and meet other signalers, but speed is ecology and movement style, not the entire move fantasy. A Wayline courier tradition could have learned to read their displays without making the creatures literal mounts or gear. This is proposed lore, not yet canon.

| **Coalcrest** | Broadest/deepest torso, long neck, heavy four-footed stance, elevated head/crest and orange line spanning a larger body. | The mature form can hold a patterned heat signal longer and broadcast it through crest/throat, or concentrate it into a damaging flash. It need not be a marathon-themed combat specialist. | `Crest Signal` and `Longfire Burst` become its signature patterned and sustained releases. `Furnace Bolt` should emerge from the visible throat/crest heat path, not an invented chest furnace. `Plate Rake` needs visual review because the approved form reads more as claw/crest than plated blade; a future name such as **Crest Rake** may fit better. **What remains uncertain:** The orange zigzag may be pigmentation, bioluminescence, exposed heat, or a mixture; the art alone does not decide. The proposed signal biology gives it a purpose while preserving Preston's preferred design. Avoid electricity/circuit jargon, shoulder pads, detachable gear, literal relay batons, canine behavior, and extra heat sacs. The line may still run or roam, but movement is one behavior among others.

### Fusern → Slagglass → Vitrecrag: form, guard, and cast glass

The images show a compact faceted low-shelled creature growing into layered, asymmetrical vitrified shelves and finally a massive buttressed carapace. Warm orange recesses sit beneath dark material. The connected underside and intended six-legged arthropod structure distinguish the line from armored reptiles. Vitrecrag's orange-gold molten glass as a weapon is Preston's direct direction; the earlier stages should lead to it without firing miniature versions of the final beam.

**Proposed ability:** these creatures take in mineral-rich sediment and heat it within protected chambers. Their shell grows by depositing and annealing material; later stages can direct still-molten glass through guarded openings. This is a fantasy biological explanation, not a claim about real-world arthropods or glass chemistry. It gives the stages different control: **Fusern seals; Slagglass opens and shapes; Vitrecrag casts and projects.**

Do not invent a turret, large hinge, or Vitrecrag-scale glass weapon. |

Keep plates attached and head direction consistent. |
| **Vitrecrag** | Huge stacked dark shell, broad buttress legs, low face, orange furnace/glass chamber and protected openings. | Has the largest reservoir and enough mass to hold pressure while **extruding a connected molten-glass beam** or firing **one heavy cannon-like cast**. Cooling should turn the glass from white/yellow-orange core to darker amber/plum at the target. | `Molten Glass` is a short extrusion; `Molten Beam` is sustained connection; `Plate Lunge` is a grounded shell/leg contact; `Molten Cannon` launches a separate weighty mass with recoil and cooling impact. Beam and cannon must have different silhouettes, timing, and aftermath. No machine barrel or generic fireball. |

**What remains uncertain:** The exact intake, chamber, and outlet anatomy are proposed. The front sprite supports a protected internal glow, but it does not prove a mechanical furnace lattice or a specific nozzle. Animate only openings already legible in the approved art; if a move needs new anatomy, revise the design with Preston rather than quietly painting hardware onto the sprite. The official Vitrecrag front and rear were approved earlier but are currently missing at their referenced game paths; restore those exact sources before an in-game visual claim.

## Production consequences

1. The first *necessary* animation for a contact move is whole-sprite travel and a target-specific hit effect. Optional body poses should be generated only if the live strike lacks clarity. This preserves the approved form and the user's chosen battle feel.
2. The Attack Forge should emphasize readable anticipation and release for ranged/body actions. The Effect Forge owns emitted heat, glass, slash, beam continuity, and target cooling. Neither Forge should invent organs that the approved sprite lacks.
3. Generate the Kithflare line's heat-signal **propagation** as a short sequence through the existing orange marking before spending quota on more running poses. This directly tests whether the new ability reading looks right to Preston.
4. Revise candidate move names and kits only after visual review. The current four move resources per form remain playable prototypes; this note changes their design interpretation, not live damage, types, or creature data.
