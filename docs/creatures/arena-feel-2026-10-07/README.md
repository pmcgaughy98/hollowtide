# Arena control model, feel and readability (Claude, 2026-10-07, evening)

## Preston's control decision (2026-10-07)
Asked whether moving should cancel the creature's AI (the Gemini report's top frustration in hybrid control), Preston answered: **"The AI controls creature movements but player can choose when to activate signature move and what enemy creatures to target."** Built as:
- Your creature's AI does all footwork and uses its kit, like every other creature.
- **Left/right** cycle your target among living enemies (sorted left to right); **up** hands the choice back to the AI. Your creature's AI aims every move at your target while it stands, and the signature (K) goes to it.
- A **marker** shows who your creature is going for: a ring at its feet and a chevron bobbing over its head, red when you chose it, white when the AI did. The HUD prints `TARGET  NAME` above your box, with `(AUTO)` when it is the AI's pick.
- WASD/arrows no longer steer. The old steering stays only for the recorded test movies, which set `debug_input`.

## Feel, from the research (`docs/research/gemini-arena-fighter-research-adoption-2026-10-07.md`)
- **Hitstop:** on a hit both creatures hold for 0.07 s (about 4 frames at 60), 0.15 s for hits of 20 or more, slams and signatures. Effects keep moving. The one that was hit shivers one pixel along the blow.
- **Telegraphs:** attacks wind up before they land, and the wind-up shows on the ground. Red ring round a melee attacker (claw, Cinder Claw, Club Slam: 0.25 to 0.35 s); red ring under the target of a lobbed or rooting move (Seed Pod, Root Snare, Riptide: 0.35 to 0.4 s); red line with a "!" over the target for a shot (Ember Shot, Pressure Jet, Deep Jet, Wick Bolt, Vent Barrage: 0.3 to 0.35 s); **yellow** line for Crest Ram (0.45 s, cannot be interrupted). The shape fills as the moment nears. Paralysis or a dazzle interrupts a red wind-up (label "Interrupted", 0.8 s before it tries again). Dodges, dashes, Sinkhole, Lantern Flash and Root Wall stay instant. Phase and Flashstep AI now also read a telegraph aimed at them in its last 0.12 s.
- **Shake cap:** ordinary hits ask for 2 map pixels (6 on screen), nothing exceeds 6 (18 on screen); the signatures used to ask for 9. **V** turns shake off.

## Readability (Preston, 2026-10-07: "the smoke and black circles cover the creature for too long or entirely")
- Scorches, cracks, embers, Riptide and the whirlpools were drawn on the effects layer **above** the creatures. They now draw on the floor layer under them, so a creature standing in a scorch is never hidden.
- The scorch is smaller (0.45 of the burst instead of 0.6), flatter and lighter (alpha 0.3, was 0.45), and fades from 2 s.
- Smoke: a quarter of the puffs (size/5, was size/2.5), each shorter lived (0.6 to 1.1 s, was 1.0 to 1.8), thinner (alpha 0.22 and 0.28, was 0.35 and 0.4) and rising faster, so the plume clears the sprite inside half a second.
- Stills: `seedpod_0_4s.png` (hit flash and one puff still cross Kithflare), `seedpod_1_0s.png` (clear, faint oval scorch under it).

## The floating pass (2026-10-09: "Warden looks like it's floating")
The cause: a creature's sprite anchor was computed once, from the lowest opaque pixel of the facing it spawned with, and never again. Facings and walk frames have different bottoms (the Warden's vary by 6 px, Windcut's walks by 9, Canvaswing's by 13), so a creature turned or walking sat above its shadow. Now every texture carries its own feet row (the lowest row with a few opaque pixels, so a claw tip or a tail pixel does not set where it stands), the anchor follows the picture on every swap, and the shadow and team ring are sized to the body's width. Check: `tools/debug/arena_anchor_check.gd` walks all 1944 textures of every form; the feet row sits on the origin within a pixel everywhere except Scourmaw, which hovers on purpose. Still: `docs/creatures/arena-anchor-2026-10-09/warden_windcut.png`.

## Abilities on 1-4, MOBA style (Preston, 2026-10-07, late)

Dodges and dashes fire at once; attacks telegraph like everyone else's. A move can have **charges**, each on its own cooldown. An enemy creature's AI fires everything. ## Kit Sense: the AI reads its kit and its team (2026-10-08)
**Preston:** a combat AI "that understands how to use its kit and synergise so the player doesn't have to worry too much", built the way he asked: read a shipped game's internal structure (Valve's Dota 2 bot layer and an open-source bot on it, through Firecrawl), take the shape, make our own. What was taken and what is ours: `docs/research/combat-ai-references-2026-10-08.md`.
- **Modes, every think:** finish (a kill is on the table within 130 px: an enemy whose HP and shield are under what our ready moves do together), peel (an ally in trouble, for Protector, Tank and Bruiser), kite (not a tank, a blow coming or under half), hold (Tank or Bruiser with the enemy standing in our terrain), engage. Named desires (0.25 low, 0.5 moderate, 0.75 high, 0.9 very high), highest wins; finish and peel retarget; kite and peel steer the footwork.
- **Desires per move:** the old "wants" rule gates (0.5 for an ability, 0.4 for the basic), then kill-secure (0.9), a held target with more hold left than our wind-up (x1.4), the team's called target (x1.2), the mode (kite: mobility x1.5, damage x0.7; peel: knockbacks and shields x1.5; finish: damage x1.2), and a veto on anything the target would phase through. The highest fires.
- **The called target:** when an enemy is newly held (stun, root, daze, Glassed), it is its opponents' call for 2 s; everyone but the protector within 160 px goes for it.
- **The AI explains itself:** every decision is logged ("Starward: peel: Counter Jab (1.00) on Rampartroot"); **L** shows the last eight lines in the arena.
- Check (`tools/debug/arena_kitsense_check.gd`): a rooted Vitrecrag became the call and 3 of 3 teammates switched to it; Stormwick in finish mode pounced an 8 HP enemy at desire 1.00 and dropped it; a Scourmaw at a third of its HP with a claw coming chose Phase in kite mode; Starward with a hurt ally under attack chose Counter Jab in peel mode and landed 14 px from the ally. An AI 3v3 (Stormwick's team against Ironvent's) ran to the 90 s cap with 34 calls and every mode used. Stills `kitsense_*.png`.
- **Grok:** the thresholds (combo-damage kill rule, half-HP kite, 2 s call, the multipliers) are drafts. The duel table before Kit Sense: `arena-balance-2026-10-08.md`; the rerun after it is queued.

## Combat upgrades (Preston, 2026-10-08: "new mechanics, animations, everything you can")
Built the way Preston set out the same hour: read a shipped game's structure first (Firecrawl), take the shape, make our own, refine for Hollowtide. Each piece names its source in `docs/research/combat-references-2026-10-08.md`.
- **Essence combos** (after Genshin's elemental reactions; our statuses are the auras): **Shatter** (Stone on Glassed: x1.5, the glass gone), **Kindle** (Ember on rooted: x1.3, roots gone), **Quench** (Tide on smouldering: x1.3, a 0.5 s daze, smoulder gone), **Conduct** (Electric on a target in a riptide: half the hit arcs to everyone else in the water), **Overgrow** (Bloom on a target in water: rooted 0.6 s). Each needs two lines to set up. Read off the attacker's last move. Kit Sense wants a move x1.3 more when its combo is on the table (Jade Lance 0.73 on a Glassed enemy against 0.56 on a clean one). Check: 20 Stone on Glassed did 30; 20 Ember on rooted did 26; 10 Tide on smouldering did 13 and dazed; the Electric hit in a riptide took 20 and 10 from the other enemy in it; the Bloom hit in water rooted 0.6 s.
- **Procedural animation layer** on every form, no new art: a wind-up lean back (3 px, stretched: scale 0.98 by 1.02 a third of the way in), a strike lunge (7 px toward the target, squashed 1.05 by 0.95 at the peak), a flinch (4 px away) on every hit, a landing squash after feints, vaults and counter jabs, and the **KO beat** (0.3 s hitstop, a white ring, a "KO" label, the drop). Scaled about the feet.
- **Crowd control after League's taxonomy:** **airborne** (Slab Vortex and Rail Recoil's blast launch; a launched creature cannot move or act, loses its ram or tackle, ground hazards miss it; 0.42 s in the check, the glass pool under it took nothing); **tenacity** (Tanks shed stuns, roots and dazes 1.4x faster: 0.30 left against 0.50 after half a second); **unstoppable** (a creature mid-ram ignores pushes: 0 px).
- **Knockback that grows** (after Smash): a push grows by up to 60% as the target's HP falls; the same push moved Ironvent 6 px at full HP and 19 px at a quarter, where it became a carry.
- **The timed team match and the Crest** (after Unite, whose page 404'd; structure unverified): team fights run 120 s with the clock at the top; when it runs out the team with more HP left wins **on points** (no more draws). **The Crest** (placeholder name) is the centre circle: a team holding it alone charges its finals' meters (6 a second: 0 to 6 in the check) and mends 1 HP every half second; contested or empty it does nothing; the ring lights in the holder's colour. Kit Sense's crest mode walks an idle final to it.
