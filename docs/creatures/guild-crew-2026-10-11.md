# The Wayline Hall's crew (Claude's proposal, approved by Preston 2026-10-11)

Preston approved the list and the art on 2026-10-11, after swapping Sky's sentry pair (a footprint-reading
door watch and a door caller, too close to Sky) for two courier jobs of our own: the flood watch and the
parcel sorter. Stillwade is a working label until Preston names the heron.

Preston: "We don't have the guild member npcs yet right and their roles? ... Make crew members and their
stages, whether they are stage 1, 2 and 3." **Names, looks and essences are proposals for Preston.**
Numbers stay Grok's. None of these lines is met in a dungeon (same rule as the townsfolk,
`docs/creatures/npc-roles-2026-10-10.md`).

**Synthesis, structure only.** Sky staffs its guild by seniority. The master is fully grown. The senior
crew run the jobs that keep the place going: the deputy, the cook. The apprentices are
first-stage creatures at the hero's own level, so the guild reads as a place where you grow up. Its
jobs map onto its rooms: a cook in the mess hall, apprentices in the crew room, the master behind a door. Hollowtide keeps that shape
with its own jobs and Missouri river creatures, like the townsfolk and the Shortcut Crew.

**Stage rule proposed for the guild:** Guildmaster stage 3, senior crew stage 2, juniors stage 1.
This changes the 2026-10-10 note that town and guild roles are all stage 3s. The townsfolk already mix
stages (Wayling 1, Swaphoard 2), so this follows what exists.

**The roll call** (built after the crew has art): each morning before a run, Wayling calls the roll on the
mark, the crew lines up round it, Starward comes out of the chamber for the word of the day, and the crew
splits to its posts. It gives every crew member one line a day. A short Wayline creed, like Sky's guild
vows, is Preston's to write or skip.

**Art, once the list is approved:** six new creatures (Grubbit, Stillwade, Rinsepaw, Wickglow, Muskin,
Slipjaw). Retro Diffusion front takes, two each, 96 px for stage 1 and 112 for stage 2. At the
2026-10-10 rate of about $0.18 a take, that is about $2.16 (12 takes). A walk sheet only for those who
move in the roll call, priced before it is run.
