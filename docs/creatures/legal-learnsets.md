# Legal learnsets

Proposal, 2026-10-02. Not live. No creature file or move file was changed.

This is `shared-move-pool.md` turned around per body. A move is legal when it is a passing signature in `move-body-gate.md` and the body has every part it needs. Ultimates are not legal for anyone else, and they are not repeated in the legal lines below. Four equipped slots stay. A longer legal line is the menu, not a fifth equipped move.

A later stage may learn an earlier stage's signature when the parts match. It does not learn that stage's ultimate.

## Cinderhide

Equipped, all legal: Ember Flick, Vent Rake, Coal Spit, Spineflare.

Also legal: Cinder Claw.

No fused brow, so no Brow Shear. The brief says this form cannot sustain a large discharge, so no Vent Row, Vent Barrage, or Slagburst. No heavytail, so no Clinker Toss. No crest, so no Crest Ram and no Furnace Roar.

## Clinkerhide

Equipped, all legal: Brow Shear, Clinker Toss, Vent Row, Slagburst.

Also legal: Ember Flick, Vent Rake, Coal Spit, Cinder Claw.

No channels, so no Vent Barrage. No ridge, so no Spineflare. No crest, so no Furnace Roar.

## Ironvent

Equipped: Crest Ram, Cinder Claw, Vent Barrage, and Furnace Roar. The first three are legal. Furnace Roar is its ultimate.

Also legal: Ember Flick, Vent Rake, Coal Spit, Spineflare, Brow Shear, Clinker Toss, Vent Row, Slagburst, Crest Signal.

Crest Signal is legal beside Crest Ram. One is a broadcast and the other is a contact strike. The kit already has the contact. Do not equip both unless a slot opens.

## Kithflare

Equipped, all legal: Signal Spark, Tail Brake, Shoulder Kindle, Handoff Dash.

Also legal: Relay Arc, Shoulder Drive, Tail Shutter, Wayflare, Furnace Bolt.

## Wayburn

Equipped, all legal: Relay Arc, Shoulder Drive, Tail Shutter, Wayflare.

Also legal: Signal Spark, Tail Brake, Shoulder Kindle, Handoff Dash, Furnace Bolt.

## Coalcrest

Equipped and legal: Crest Signal, Furnace Bolt, Longfire Burst.

Plate Rake is equipped and illegal. It needs a shell. Leave the file alone until Preston wants the kit changed.

Also legal: Crest Ram, Signal Spark, Tail Brake, Shoulder Kindle, Handoff Dash, Relay Arc, Shoulder Drive, Tail Shutter, Wayflare.

The failed slot is 50 power and 4 uses. Two legal moves sit on that rung and use a part the rest of the kit does not: Shoulder Kindle uses the marking, and Tail Shutter uses the splittail. Tail Shutter's name overclaims a shutter. Either can fill the slot. Neither is equipped by this proposal.

## Fusern

Equipped, all legal: Seam Spark, Hex Cut, Ember Pellet, Kilnburst.

Nothing else in the pool is legal. The hex edge and the narrow seam are this body's. It does not learn the shelf moves or Plate Lunge. No chamber and no mouth, so no Molten Cannon and no Molten Beam.

## Slagglass

Equipped, all legal: Glass Spur, Shield Flare, Furnace Shard, Glassburst.

Nothing else in the pool is legal. The lifting shelf is this body's. It does not learn Fusern's seam or hex moves, and it does not learn Plate Lunge. Furnace Shard's name overclaims a furnace. The hit stays a shelf move. No chamber, so no Molten Cannon and no Molten Beam.

## Vitrecrag

Equipped and legal: Molten Beam, Plate Lunge, Molten Cannon. Molten Glass is its ultimate. Beam needs the chamber and the mouth. Leave it equipped. No new Beam art.

Nothing else in the pool is legal. Shield Flare is a lifting shelf, and Hex Cut is a hexagonal edge. Vitrecrag has neither. Plate Lunge stays its shell move because the front plate is driven by buttress legs.

## No legal signature

Rimefin has a fin and a jaw. Nothing in the pool uses either. Strike stays its only move. A Tide jaw strike is the honest addition, and it has no name here.

Oxbowarchon has a rail. Kingstone Rail is its ultimate. No shared Stone move exists. Slab is still not a part.

Punchybun has a fist, a ferret tail, and the star. Nothing in the pool uses them. A fist contact has no name here. The star shield stays its ultimate, and it is not drawn as a move yet.
