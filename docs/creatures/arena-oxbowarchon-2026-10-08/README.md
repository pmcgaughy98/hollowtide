Preostmar, Oathfen, Oxbowarchon. **Preston, 2026-10-08:** no longer his personal line; Oxbowarchon is one of the **Gemstone Kings**, a pseudo-legendary like Ironvent. The three data files now carry Bloom first and Stone second, and the Kingstone Rail move is Bloom (`data/creatures/901..903`, `data/moves/oxbowarchon_kingstone_rail.tres`). **Grok:** those are Preston's words; the rest of the line's turn-based data (stats, kits) is still yours.

Basic Jade Lance (AI). **1 Ground Spikes** (14, range 120, area telegraph): jade erupts under the target and holds it 0.8 s. **2 Stone Cocoon:** the slabs pull tight for 1.2 s (damage x0.4, cannot be moved or carried, planted), then burst outward as shrapnel (12) into everyone within 44 px. **3 Slab Vortex** (15, 40 px): it rears and spins its plates; everything close is struck and thrown back. **Passive, Bound Slabs:** two jade slabs float at its hips (drawn, bound by a seam of light); each swallows one blow of 6 or more ("Slab") and a new one is pulled in after 7 s.

The target takes the signature damage; **everyone else within 48 px takes half, is floored (dazed) and thrown back; the crater stays ringed with Kingstone shards for 7 s** that slow enemies to 0.6 and shut off their movement abilities (enemy-only terrain, like the glass and the seams). ## Backline rework (Preston, 2026-10-08, later)
**Preston:** "Oxbowarchon will be more of a backline character, playing on the railgun wherever it makes sense tactically for a railgun. Make his kit supplement that too." So the Wall above is gone; Oxbowarchon is **ARTILLERY**: it keeps its distance (the kite profile, about 90 px in the check), and everything it fires hits x1.3 from beyond 110 px.
- **Basic Jade Lance** (AI), range 110.
- **1 Rail Shot** (placeholder name): a 0.7 s red line telegraph out to 220 px, then the rails fire a jade slug down the whole line that **pierces everything on it**; each creature hit is knocked along the line, and Oxbowarchon recoils a step. Best down a line with two enemies on it.
- **2 Ground Spikes:** jade erupts under whatever closes in and holds it 0.8 s: its peel.
- **3 Rail Recoil** (placeholder name): it fires both rails at the floor; the recoil vaults it 70 px away from the nearest enemy (clamped by the floor and rocks), untouchable for 0.3 s, and the blast throws that enemy off (8).
- **Passive Bound Slabs** as before; **4 Kingstone Rail** as before. Stone Cocoon and Slab Vortex move down to Preostmar and Oathfen, which still brawl with the slabs.

## Also this slice: the AI weighs abilities before the basic attack
In `_update_ai` a creature now considers its abilities first and its basic attack last. Before, a basic that is nearly always ready (Vitrecrag's Plate Lunge, Molten Beam) crowded the abilities out, and the AI Vitrecrag never guarded in a 41 s fight; after, it guarded twice. This changes every AI kit's rhythm a little. **Grok:** worth a look in the simulator.
