## Completed Run 1: Vitrecrag true back (historical prompt)

**Input:** approved `docs/HollowTide Sprites/Creatures/Vitrecrag.jfif` / `assets/sprites/creatures/vitrecrag_official.png` as the exact same-form front. The old `docs/HollowTide Sprites/Creatures-Back/Vitrecrag-Back.jfif` can guide camera angle only if the Forge accepts a second reference. The approved front has priority.

```text
TASK: SAME-FORM BACK. VIEW: TRUE THREE-QUARTER REAR. FORM: Vitrecrag. Derive the exact same fortress beetle from its APPROVED updated front, not a new design. The viewer stands behind it; the broad high rear shell, back of its six thick planted legs, and overlapping plates dominate. The front of its body descends LOWER and AWAY from the higher rear. Hide the face, eye, and mouth completely. Its uneven shell is made of a few LARGE irregular STACKED plates, not a horn, upward spike, even tile grid, smooth round turtle dome, or added equipment. Keep a substantial orange furnace opening on the creature's RIGHT flank, recessed under the plate overhang and visible from this rear angle. Dark plum glass/stone, soot, rust joints and restrained furnace orange must match the approved front. Use the same coarse logical pixel density, broad material clusters and stepped shading as the front. Native 128x128 square with transparent final export; if the Forge requires magenta, use only flat #FF00FF outside the silhouette. One whole creature, planted feet, no floor shadow, no text or extra parts. Reject if this reads as another side/front view or shows a face.
```

The detailed rejection list is in [Vitrecrag's rear brief](../creatures/vitrecrag-back-imagegen-brief.md). Do not link any output until Preston approves it.

## Completed Run 2: Wayburn Stage II front redo (historical prompt)

```text

Keep its earless short triangular head, slit eye, cream angular throat wedge, rust zigzag, four reptilian three-toed legs and split counterweight tail. Make a clear intermediate adult: deeper chest and ribcage, taller spring-loaded hips, longer neck, wider pelvis and stronger forelimbs than Kithflare, but leaner and less massive than the approved final-form Coalcrest. The pose MUST differ from Coalcrest's long horizontal broadside stance: turn Wayburn's chest and head partly toward the viewer while still facing right, bend the torso in a braking curve, lift one near forefoot clearly off the ground, plant the other three at different depths, and arc the tail LOW and back instead of straight up horizontally. Leave the final-form head crest absent; only a low hide ridge may begin. Occupy about 82x76 cells in the same 100x100 logical grid and common foot baseline. Use broad charcoal/rust planes, the pale throat and internal heat seams with Cinderhide-level coherent pixel clusters. Reject a four-feet-planted side profile, Coalcrest's exact pose or straight raised tail, unchanged Kithflare crouch, simple scaling, extra shoulder armor or heat sacs, a dog, wolf, otter or horse face. One whole creature, flat #FF00FF magenta outside it for conversion.
```

Compare the result with **both** approved Kithflare and approved Coalcrest at native game scale. Wayburn must read as the anatomical and visual bridge with its own action pose. Preston chooses it before importing or generating its back.
