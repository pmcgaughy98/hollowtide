# Attack Forge briefs for the nine made Ember creatures

These are one-run-at-a-time **Motion Briefs** for the [Hollowtide Attack Animation Forge](https://opal.google/edit/1mcPSsql5N9rj53Nbg0FFqFEh-e39c_f-). They cover the four moves currently equipped by each of the nine generated Ember forms. Rimefin is not in this batch: its Forge redraw and later stages still need approved art. The names come from the live `data/creatures/` and `data/moves/` resources, not the older move-set draft.

The [appearance and ability reassessment](../creatures/ember-creature-physiology-and-action-brief-2026-09-27.md) is the design companion. In particular, the Kithflare line's connected orange marking is now the proposed source of patterned heat signals; running is a visible movement trait, not its defining supernatural ability. The underlying signal biology still needs Preston's approval.

**How to use:** Upload the named creature's approved exact-view image as Source Sprite. For the first run, use its front. Paste **one numbered prompt** into Motion Brief and press Play. Leave Existing Attack Sheet empty for `MODE: NEW`. Save the raw output and review before another run. The Forge makes a 3×2, six-pose **creature body** sheet; emitted material and target contact belong in the Effect Forge. F0 is the approved rest pose, F4 is the named event, and F5 returns toward rest. Keep one camera, one native logical grid, one fixed foot origin, a flat magenta exterior, and no labels or target creature.

**Current combat direction:** For a **CONTACT** move, Godot carries the intact sprite toward the opponent and plays strike art at contact. Its body-sheet prompt below is optional polish, not a prerequisite. Keep body motion within a fixed cell; never draw the creature traveling across the sheet. When quota is tight, run one priority **BODY** prompt per creature first. A generated sheet is only a candidate, not a live animation.

**Back view later:** Repeat an accepted front action with `VIEW: BACK` and the exact form's approved battle back as Source Sprite. Preserve the move's six beat functions, rear anatomy, correct left/right asymmetry, camera, and native grid; never mirror the front or reveal a face. Ironvent's current back is provisional and Clinkerhide's back is an unapproved candidate, so hold those back runs. Vitrecrag uses its approved 128×128 logical grid. The other forms keep their approved native grids rather than forcing 64×64.

## Selection and review

Start with one priority BODY prompt per creature, then compare the native cells against its approved source. For Vitrecrag, compare Beam and Cannon separately because their release physics must differ. A sheet passes only if F0 matches the approved form, F1–F5 keep the same camera and foot origin, F4 reads as the requested event, and no frame invents parts or changes pixel density. Use `MODE: COMPLETE` only when an actual partial sheet exists: upload it, name exactly which F slots to **KEEP** and **MISSING**, and review kept pixels afterward. Opal cannot guarantee exact copies.

Contact moves already have a playable whole-sprite direction; spend generation quota on their optional body poses only after their movement and target effect have been reviewed in battle. Front and back sheets remain separate jobs. None of these prompts automatically changes `MoveData`, the battle renderer, or creature canon.
