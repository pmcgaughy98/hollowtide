# Hollowtide: first public demo plan

Claude, 2026-10-05, at Preston's request. A proposal: every scope number below is Preston's to change.

## What to call it

| Term | What it means | Where Hollowtide is |
|---|---|---|
| Proof of concept | The core loop works at all | Roughly here: walk, encounter, battle, catch, evolve, route all exist |
| **Vertical slice / demo** | 20 to 40 minutes that look and play like the finished game | **The target.** This is what players, artists, programmers and publishers judge |
| Alpha / beta | The whole game playable, then polished | Much later |
| Early access | A paid, unfinished release that keeps growing | Not first. It needs far more content and an audience already waiting |

So the goal is a **free vertical slice demo**, released first on itch.io (free, fast feedback), and later on a Steam page to collect wishlists.

## What the demo contains

One path, start to finish, with a clear ending:

menu → choose courier → hometown → Route 1 → bridge crossing → riverfront historic block → **one capstone battle** (a rival or route boss) → ending card ("thanks for playing", wishlist and community links).

## The pitch kit

What brings people in, by who you want:

- **Players:** the free demo, a 60 to 90 second trailer (lead with a signature move), 5 to 8 short GIFs, a Discord.
- **Artists and programmers:** the same, plus a one-page pitch: the hook, what's built, what's needed, and what you offer. Put any revenue-share deal in writing before work starts.
- **Publishers and investors:** a vertical slice, a short pitch deck, and evidence of interest (wishlists, demo downloads, community size). Monster-taming comparisons they'll know: Coromon, Nexomon, Cassette Beasts, Temtem.

The one-page pitch's pillars, as a draft: the courier fantasy; a river-town world drawn from St. Joseph; one-to-one evolution lines; moves that follow the body; cinematic signature moves.

## Risks to settle before going public

- **AI-made art.** Steam asks developers to disclose generative AI use, and some players, artists and publishers are wary of it. Confirm the commercial terms for Retro Diffusion, PixelLab and Opal outputs, and decide how you'll describe the art pipeline.
- **Licences.** The fonts are OFL (fine). Check every music and sound source.
- **Punchybuns.** It's a memorial for a real person. Include it in a public demo only if Gabrielle agrees.
- **Similarity.** Keep creatures and UI clearly unlike Pokémon. The essence system and courier framing already help.
