## Vitrecrag

```text
TASK: REDRAW. VIEW: FRONT. FORM: Stage III Vitrecrag. Attach the current Vitrecrag front in Previous Stage Sprite, and approved Cinderhide in Style Anchor. Preston likes Vitrecrag's creature type and overall design: preserve its recognizable low armored face, forward crest, stacked dark-plum/slag shell, glowing furnace lattice and right-facing fortress-arthropod silhouette. Its field form should suggest a creature roughly 12–15 m high and 18–22 m along the shell (working research range, not final canon); battle scale is compressed. Rework the six legs so they visibly join the underside and descend as thick load-bearing buttresses, with the nearest foot broad and planted. Enrich the existing shell with stepped broken-glass strata, soot pockets, copper seams and Cinderhide-level shadow/midtone/highlight clusters; keep the detail organized in large planes rather than noise. Retain the orange furnace as an internal chamber. A *small controlled tongue* of heat at the forward vent may remain from the current image, but the plume must not dominate the silhouette. Keep the head small beneath the carapace and the asymmetrical high shield-back. Palette charcoal, brick, dark plum glass, tarnished copper and restrained orange. Avoid redesigning it into a new species, pink crystal, smooth shell, thin dangling legs, floating feet, crab claws, machinery or an oversized flame fan. Its weight and material finish must read beside Cinderhide in battle.
```

## Kithflare

```text
VIEW: FRONT. FORM: Stage I Kithflare. TASK: NEW. Previous Stage Sprite empty; Cinderhide as Style Anchor only. Design a heat-sensing relay courser with a long horizontal ribcage, high coiled rear hocks, two short bracing forelegs and a counterweight tail. Its broad, flat triangular head has a hard beak-like jaw edge, side-set slit eye, no ears, no cheeks and no rounded nose. Four distinct three-toed feet visibly connect to the torso. One cream angular throat mark, one rust zigzag flank mark and a tiny fork at the tail end carry lineage identity. Soot charcoal and rust, only a few ember pixels in flush throat/rib seams. Three-quarter right-facing launch crouch with rear legs compressed and one forefoot planted forward; alert, mature, fast. Use roughly 68x60 logical cells, giving it a substantial Stage I body rather than a tiny pet. Avoid dog, otter, wolf, horse, mustelid, puppy eyes, paws, fur, rounded muzzle, upright raptor, rider, tack, heat sacs, shoulder pads, plates, external flames and cute proportions.
```
