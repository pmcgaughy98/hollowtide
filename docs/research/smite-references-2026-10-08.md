# Smite references: kit synergy and balance (Claude, 2026-10-08)

**Preston:** "Smite I think is a great reference for how I want my kits to synergize and the balance of the game." And on the Punchybuns line: "a bit of the playstyle I want for Punchybuns line is like Ravana, the passive and the basic attacking punching at least."

Read through Firecrawl as structures, nothing copied. Smite's gods are the arena's shape already: a passive, three abilities and an ultimate. Each section names the source, what was taken, and what was not.

## 1. Ravana, for the Punchybuns line
Source: [Ravana, Smite Wiki](https://smite.fandom.com/wiki/Ravana). The structure: his passive is a **combo chain**. Every basic attack or ability that lands adds to it; each time it reaches a count he gets a **shield worth a share of his max health**, some speed and some power, stacking three times; the third basic in his hit chain **cleaves**. His abilities are a projected punch that slows (a cone), a kick that makes him immune and cleanses, a flurry that roots the first target and heals him per enemy hit, and a leap that makes enemies take more damage and him take less.

What we took, now: **the basic is a three-punch chain** (Spark Jab, cooldown 0.7 s): two jabs, then a **Hook** that goes through everyone in front within reach (x1.3, a push), the chain lapsing after 2 s without a punch. **The Chain** counts every hit the line lands (punches and abilities); at eight (stage 2 and 3, as the passive "chain") it **pays out**: a shield worth 7% of max HP on the ferret and on every ally beside it (40 px, Guardian's old reach), stacking to three shields, and 2 s of haste. Guardian's flat 15% cut is folded into the payout shields, so the protector still protects the people it loves, but now by punching. Stage 1 counts and hooks but does not pay out (the stage rule). What was not taken yet: the four abilities. The fit, for a later pass: the projected punch could replace Static Dance's charge as a ranged jab, the immune kick is close to Counter Jab, the rooting flurry is a stronger Counter Jab, the leap with a damage-taken debuff is a different Starshield; Preston decides. **Grok:** 8, 7%, three stacks, x1.3 and 0.7 s are drafts.

## 2. Crowd control and diminishing returns
Source: [Crowd Control, Smite Wiki](https://smite.fandom.com/wiki/Crowd_Control). The structure: three tiers. **Soft** CC makes an action harder (slow, cripple, blind, vortex) and neither applies nor is affected by diminishing returns; slows stack with reduced efficiency above 40%. **Hard** CC makes an action impossible (stun, root, silence, disarm, fear, taunt) and applies a stack of **diminishing returns**: each stack gives 20% crowd-control reduction for 15 s, up to four stacks (80%). **True hard** CC (knockback, knockup, pull, grab, banish) ignores diminishing returns and applies two stacks. Cleanse items and short immunities sit on top.

What we took: **diminishing returns** on hard holds. The first time a creature is stunned, rooted, dazed or launched it adds a stack; every hold that starts inside the next 15 s is shortened by 20% per stack, to four stacks ("Resisting"). It sits beside tenacity (Tanks shed holds faster) and the two compound. Already matching: our taxonomy after League (slow and cripple soft; stun, root, daze, hush hard; airborne, carries and the rail's floor as the true hard tier). Not taken: slow stacking maths (one slow at a time here), the cleanse item (Starshield already cleanses), fear, taunt, madness, polymorph.

## 3. Protections, penetration and the balance axis
Source: [Attack and Defense, Smite Wiki](https://smite.fandom.com/wiki/Attack_and_Defense). The structure: damage is physical or magical and each has its own **protection** stat; mitigation is `damage = 100 x unmitigated / (protection + 100)`, so 50 protection cuts a third and 100 cuts half; penetration and reduction peel protection back in a fixed order; percentage damage reduction stacks on top and cannot be penetrated; healing has anti-heal as its counter; basic attacks are skillshots with a hit progression.

What we took: nothing new in code. The arena's second axis is the essence chart (x1.5 and x1.25 strong, x0.75 and x0.9 weak), and the role layer's flat cuts (Rampart, Guardian, Windbreak, Cocoon, Glass Guard) behave like Smite's percentage reduction, which stacks and cannot be penetrated. A protections stat would be a third axis on top of essence and role, and Grok owns whether the roster wants one. Already matching: the Damage role's wound is Smite's anti-heal; the three-punch chain is a hit progression.

## 4. Classes
The Classes page came back as a table too large to parse cleanly (the god lists); the five class roles (Assassin, Guardian, Hunter, Mage, Warrior) and their team jobs are known shape: the Guardian sets up with hard CC and the Mage or Hunter pays off, the Warrior holds the solo lane as a bruiser, the Assassin takes the jungle and picks off. Our seven roles sit on that: Tank and Protector as Guardians, Bruiser as the Warrior, Skirmisher and Assassin as Assassins, Artillery as the Hunter or Mage, Controller as a CC Guardian, Shelter as a support Guardian. The set-up-and-pay-off chain is what Kit Sense's combo desires and called targets already reward; a later pass could add **class-pair bonuses** (a hold followed inside a second by a different line's hit) as the explicit synergy Preston is asking for.

## 5. Relics, for a loadout layer: Charms
Source: [Relics, Smite Wiki](https://smite.fandom.com/wiki/Relics). The structure: every player starts with an empty relic slot (and a shard slot) and fills it for free before leaving the fountain; relics are actives on long cooldowns that give every god the same small set of answers (a cleanse that breaks crowd control, a blink, an invulnerability, a reflect, a damage amp on a target, an area slow, a shield for allies, a speed boost for allies, and more), upgraded through tiers later. The point is counterplay that does not depend on the kit: anyone can carry an answer to a stun or a burst.

What we took: **Charms** (placeholder name), one per creature, chosen in the picker for yours (a "YOUR CHARM" row with a line of what each does) and by role for the AI's (tanks Thorns, bruisers and controllers Beads, skirmishers and assassins Blink, artillery Aegis, protectors Shell, the shelter Wings). C uses yours; the AI uses its own by rule (beads when held, aegis when low and pressed, blink to escape or to close on a low target, thorns when two are on it, spear on the called target, emblem when two are close, shell when an ally beside it is low and threatened, wings to close a long gap or hold the Crest). Eight charms, 45 to 60 s cooldowns: Beads (breaks every hold, 1 s immune), Blink (90 px), Aegis (1.5 s untouchable and still), Thorns (3 s, 40% back), Spear (target takes 20% more for 5 s), Emblem (45 px, 40% slow 3 s), Shell (allies within 60 px shielded 15% of max HP), Wings (allies hasted 3 s). Not taken: tiers, gold, the shard slot, teleport, the item store. **Grok:** every number is a draft; this is the balance lever Preston asked for, because it is the same for every creature.

## 6. What to Smite-ify in the kits (the audit)
Preston: "see what kits should be Smite-ified... we don't have to limit it to 5 roles or exactly the combat style." Measured against Smite's shape (every god: a passive, mobility or a hard CC, an aimed skillshot, an ultimate; everything dodgeable), line by line:
| Line | Has | Missing, or not yet Smite-shaped |
|---|---|---|
| Stormwick | escape (Ember Feint), engage (Lantern Tackle), poke (Volley), ultimate | complete |
| Rampartroot | root, slam, wall, ultimate | no mobility: fine for a guardian; its Seed lob is the only basic that is a true skillshot already |
| Scourmaw | dodge (Phase), zone (Riptide), ambush, ultimate | complete |
| Ironvent | engage (Crest Ram), poke, knock, ultimate | complete |
| Vitrecrag | lob, beam, guard, ultimate | no mobility: fine for a tank; the Glassed slow is its CC |
| Oxbowarchon | rail, spikes, escape (Rail Recoil), ultimate | complete; its basic is a line skillshot |
| Starward | dance, guard, dash (Counter Jab), ultimate; now the Ravana chain | Ravana's four abilities are open options (section 1) |
| Warden | interrupt, brace, zone, ultimate | no mobility: a controller; Smite would give it a short hop |
| Galeshear | shield, zone, lift, ultimate | no self-mobility for a glider: Lift moves it with the ally; a free glide dash is the obvious add |
The real Smite-shaping is not in the lists above but in **how abilities land**: in Smite every ability is a shape aimed by the player (cone, line, circle on the ground) that a target can step out of; ours resolve on the target when the telegraph ends. That is item 4 of the dynamism list (moves as moving hitboxes), which comes after item 3 in Preston's order, and it is where the manual control earns its keep: a steered creature aims with its facing and the AI dodge step already reads telegraphs. Until then, the charms, the chain, diminishing returns, frame data and the role layer are the Smite structures in the game.
