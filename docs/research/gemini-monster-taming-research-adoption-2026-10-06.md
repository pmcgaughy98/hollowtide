# Adoption note: "Monster Taming Game Design Research" (Gemini, 2026-10-06)

Source: Preston's Google Doc of that name (Gemini Deep Research from `gemini-monster-taming-archive-prompt-2026-10-06.md`). Read by Claude 2026-10-06. Many citations are Reddit threads and blogs, so treat numbers as rules of thumb, not measurements.

## Adopt (proposals until Preston signs off)
1. **Back views** (Claude + Codex art pipeline): angle the back 15 to 30 degrees off straight-behind so a cheek, snout or eye stays visible; put the creature's signature feature on the back (tail, lantern, crest, shell) as the focal point. Matches what works in our good backs (Scourmaw, the Kithflare line). New rule for every back: generate directly with the front as a reference (not the RD rotation), the tail and dorsal feature must be visible, and check it beside the front before install.
2. **Evolution checklist** (for Grok and RD prompts): stage by stage the head gets smaller relative to the body, the silhouette goes from round to angular or armoured, the stance from low and wide-eyed to dominant, and the theme feature escalates (spark, then crest, then a whole ridge) rather than the body just growing. This is Preston's "evolved, not grown up" direction written as rules.
3. **Reference material**: Gemini's legal section recommends real-world references (Missouri wildlife and geography) over game rips. The public-domain finds in `internet-archive-scan-2026-10-06.md` (the 1908 fish plates, St. Joseph histories) fit that.
4. **Idle secondary motion**: tails, ears and barbels lag the body's breath on a longer cycle. Worth naming in future RD idle prompts.

## Already true in Hollowtide
- Layout: foe upper right, your creature lower left and larger, full-width message box. Same as the research.
- Message box hiding the bottom of your creature: the research says 15 to 25%; ours hides 27% (Preston's 2026-09-30 decision). Close enough; no change proposed.

## Not adopted
- Signature timing (200 to 500 ms phases): that is for ordinary skill animations. Hollowtide's signatures are deliberately cinematic (the Kingstone Rail bar), so no change.
- Gen 5-style cut-out skeletal animation: Gemini itself notes rotation blurs pixel art; our frame-based RD idles avoid that.

## Flag for the demo plan (Preston's decision)
The US Copyright Office's 2023 guidance (cited in the report) says AI-generated images without meaningful human authorship can't be registered for copyright. Hollowtide's code, design, names, writing and overall arrangement are Preston's. But the individual AI-made sprites may be weakly protected unless a human reworks them. Gemini's "all production art must be made by human artists" overstates it: it is a protection question, not a ban. One practical answer: a hired pixel artist reworking the final creature art, which the demo plan's recruiting already aims at. Not legal advice; worth one consult before any commercial launch.
