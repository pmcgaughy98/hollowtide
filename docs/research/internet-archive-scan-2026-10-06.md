# Internet Archive scan (Claude, 2026-10-06)

Searched archive.org's public search API (no connector exists). Two kinds of finds: other monster games to **study** (ideas only), and public-domain material Hollowtide can **use outright**. Proposals below are not canon; mechanics questions go to Grok per the creature rules.

## Use outright: public domain (pre-1929 US works; check each item's rights line)
| Item | Year | Rights shown | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| [The fishes of Illinois](https://archive.org/details/fishesofillinois03forb) (Forbes & Richardson) | 1908 | Public domain (BHL) | Catfish, gar, paddlefish, sturgeon plates: river-creature anatomy reference for the Rimefin line and future Tide creatures |
| [Northern fishes, Upper Mississippi Valley](https://archive.org/details/northernfisheswi00eddy) | 1947 | "No known copyright restrictions" per scanner; verify before copying art | Same, regional species |
| [History of Buchanan County and St. Joseph](https://archive.org/details/historyofbuchana00rutt) | 1904 | Pre-1929 | Real districts, buildings, landmarks for routes and town layout |
| [Daily News' history of Buchanan County and St. Joseph](https://archive.org/details/cu31924028846439) | 1898 | Pre-1929 | Same, plus early settlement stories |
| [History of early steamboat navigation on the Missouri River](https://archive.org/details/historyearlyste00chitgoog) (Chittenden) | 1903 | Not in copyright | River lore, wrecks, snags, sandbars: river-route ideas |
| [The story of the pony express](https://archive.org/details/storyofponyexpre00bradrich) (Bradley) | 1913 | Not in copyright | Courier history; the Pony Express started in St. Joseph in 1860 |
| [Historic Resource Study: Pony Express National Historic Trail](https://archive.org/details/historicresource00godf) | 1994 | Public domain (US government) | Route, stations, riders |
| [Literature search, cultural resources along the Missouri](https://archive.org/details/DTIC_ADA101737) (Army Corps, DTIC) | 1978 | US government work | River-corridor history and sites |

## Study only: other monster games (copyrighted; borrow ideas, never names, art, music or text)
| Game (where on the Archive) | Idea worth learning from | Hollowtide fit (proposal) |
|---|---|---|
| Medabots (manuals, magazines) | Parts-based battles; breaking a part disables its attacks | Hollowtide already gates moves on body parts; a broken part could lock its move for the battle |
| Jade Cocoon 2 ([manual](https://archive.org/details/ps2_Jade_Cocoon_2_USA)) | Merging creatures visibly passes on body parts | Body parts as inherited, visible traits |
| Digimon World ([browser emulation](https://archive.org/details/psx_digiwld)) | How you raise a creature decides which evolution it takes | Branching evolutions by care or training |
| Monster Rancher 2 ([browser emulation](https://archive.org/details/psx_mranchr2)) | Creatures generated from real-world discs | Creatures tied to real St. Joseph places |
| Telefang (videos, fan projects) | Creatures contacted by phone numbers | A courier network: creatures reached through mail or relay stations |
| Archived Fandom wikis (Monster Tamer Wiki, Digimon World wikis; WikiTeam) | Genre-wide design notes | Research only |

Downloadable commercial game files on the Archive are often fan uploads of still-copyrighted games; stick to the browser-playable items, manuals and videos.
