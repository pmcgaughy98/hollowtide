## The three things Preston named

### Psychic lift (80-86 s, 89-93 s, 96-99 s)
- **80-81 s, grab and fling:** Mewtwo grabs Pikachu.
  - The target gets a pink outline, and a dotted white tether runs from Mewtwo's hand.
  - Purple smoke trails behind it as it is swung around and flung into Snorlax (hit spark at 81.75).
- **82-85.75 s, hammer:** Mewtwo lifts a big purple-outlined body and hammers Pikachu with it four times in about 3 s.
  - It rises with its shadow left on the floor, so the gap grows.
  - Each slam throws white burst stars out both sides and rock chips off.
  - The crater under the target deepens with each slam, and the target stays pinned in it with a pink ring.
  - To my eye the lifted body is Snorlax tinted by the aura, because Snorlax lies by the wall right after. Preston wants it done with a rock, and the mechanic is the same.
- **86.0 s, throw into the wall:** the held body is thrown into the top wall with a purple trail. A burst goes off at the wall and it lands dazed with stars.
- **89-93 s, field version:** Mewtwo glows with a purple ring under it.
  - A large mossy boulder rises beside it.
  - Then dozens of rocks of every size float up across the whole arena, each with its own shadow.
  - At 93 s they all come down on the target as one white flash inside a big ellipse.
- **96-99 s, boss version:** Mewtwo holds six creatures at once, one tether each, all outlined purple. The centre half-circle of the floor turns red.

### Wall collisions (86-88.5 s)
- **86.5 s, charge:** pink speed lines converge on Mewtwo.
- **87-87.75 s, pin:** a fat pink beam pushes Charmander across the arena into the right wall and pins it there, with sparks and dust at the contact point.
- **88-88.5 s, drag:** the beam bends and drags the target down along the wall, leaving a dark, scorched crack trail on the wall.
- **The arena edge is a solid wall with a rail.** Creatures and thrown things stop at it with an impact.

### Destroyable objects
- **11 s, ~~rolling boulder~~ Golem's Rollout (corrected 2026-10-07 from the native frames):** what looked like a grey boulder is Golem curled up, rolling with a dust trail into a wooden crate. The crate bursts into spinning plank shards and Golem keeps rolling. So this is a creature move, not an arena object.
- **52-55 s, crate break:** Jolteon dashes through a crate with a dust puff and flying planks.
  - **The wreck stays:** a pile of broken planks remains where the crate was, with shards scattered for the rest of the fight. Jolteon then stands on the pile.
- **59-60 s:** crates are set around the edges of the grass arena.
- **41-44 s:** a big boulder sits on the dirt arena. After a water twister, rock chunks and dust litter the field.
- **68-70 s:** the beam clash leaves a burning crater with radial cracks and scattered rock and plank bits.
- **18 s:** a green crystal rock is shattered by fire.

## The boulder, measured (Claude, 2026-10-07, from the 1080p video Preston linked)
Frames at 42 s and 89 s. The boulder beside Arcanine is about **2.3 creature-widths wide and 1.7 creature-heights tall**; beside Mewtwo about 2.5 heights. It is one big faceted stone: cream facets on the upper left, grey in the middle, dark grey-brown on the lower right (lit from the upper left), two or three moss patches on top, a heavy dark outline round the silhouette and thinner seams between facets, and a deep shadow on the floor. There is one per arena, against the top wall. At 44 s its debris is **big angular shards, each half a creature or more**, in the same flat facet colours with outlines, scattered over about a quarter of the field, with dust and a dark patch where it stood. The 90 s floating rocks are dirt-coloured chunks of every size, each with its own shadow.

## Gap list: what Hollowtide's arena does not have yet
- **Objects:** arena objects (rocks, crates), and debris that stays on the floor.
- **Hits:** wall and object impacts, lift and throw, and craters that stay.
- **Moves and cutaways:** beam clash, a trap bubble, clones, crowd banners on big moments, and pillars rising from the floor.
- ~~**Modes:** more than two fighters.~~ Done later on 2026-10-07: 1v1, 2v2 and 3v3 (N key). A free-for-all like the 41-44 s four-way is not built.

## Build plan (Claude proposal; steps 1 and 2 built later on 2026-10-07, see `docs/creatures/arena-ironvent-2026-10-07/README.md`; step 3 not built)
1. **Rocks.** Two to four per map. They block movement and shots and take damage.
   - They crack in two stages, then break into chunks that fly out and leave rubble on the floor for the rest of the fight.
   - Drawn in code first; Retro Diffusion art later (about $0.54 for a rock plus two crack states).
2. **Wall and rock slams.** A big hit (Club Slam, Deep Jet, a thrown rock) carries the target. If it hits the arena wall or a rock, it takes slam damage, gets dust and a short daze with stars, and leaves a crack mark on the wall.
3. **The lift.**
   - Raise the nearest rock with a tether and glow; its shadow stays on the floor.
   - Slam it on the target once or twice, leaving a crater, then throw it.
   - It shatters on a fighter, a wall or another rock.
   - **Who gets it is Preston's and Grok's call.** Claude's suggestion: Rampartroot's roots tear the rock out of the ground and heave it (a tank that shapes the ground). None of the current essences is psychic, so a true telekinetic lifter would be a future creature.
