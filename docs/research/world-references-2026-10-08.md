## Implemented playable slice

Launch and the approved menu retain their existing presentation. New Game chooses courier appearance, then enters the hometown with an empty party. Follow the signs or pin to the courier hall. The dispatcher explains the missing return mark at the old landing and asks for help with the outgoing mail. Collect spare straps, interact with each of three waiting partners, then return to the one you want and invite it. Existing front-idle frames animate the actual creatures in the room. Invitation adds exactly that creature to the run-local party. The dispatcher then gives the existing sealed pouch; the levee opens. The other two helpers remain at the hall. No new creature rules, combat mechanics or economic rewards are introduced.

The region map reveals only visited and adjacent destinations. It describes road connections, without teleportation. The hometown pass composes existing art and retains the home/hall doors, garden task and main-road exits.

## Product direction and current limits

This makes the opening understandable through play and gives the growing world a navigation layer. It does not establish parity with a shipped commercial RPG. A complete first chapter, campaign encounters/recruitment linked to the current arena, durable save/load, progression balance and player testing remain necessary. Current story, discovery and party ownership are run-local. The old route encounter controller still exists; this work does not replace it with the arena.

The seven-form refinements, original art backups, Oxbowarchon's dark jade palette, and Claude's Listener/Canvaswing work are preserved. New Galeshear wing repair is tracked separately from this world slice.
