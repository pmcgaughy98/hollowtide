## Decisions for this pass
Use varied grouped silhouettes, readable travel space, consistent native pixels, restrained motion and context-specific river vegetation as practical review criteria. These are our design judgments, not copied game assets or a claim about benchmark equivalence.

The approved cottonwood variants and sage ground atlas anchor the palette. The river continues down the screen. Root details and reeds belong near damp margins; meadow tufts remain low and sparse so signs and characters stay readable. The bridge and dispatcher architecture remain the accepted Hollowtide designs.

Initial larger repeated grass clumps and oversized roots were judged at zoom 3 and revised. Rejected placements and all original source candidates remain saved.
