# Route 1 regional references, October 2, 2026

Preston supplied these references during the roof and individual-prop pass. They guide Hollowtide's visual geography; they do not automatically make real places or proposed world names canon.

## Native trees

[EasyScape: trees native to St. Joseph, Missouri](https://easyscape.com/categories/tree?address=st-joseph-missouri&filter=native), inspected October 2. The list includes Eastern cottonwood, Plains cottonwood, American sycamore, bur oak, black willow and Eastern redbud. This pass keeps the current cottonwood model and makes individual variants from its native leaf clusters. Other local species can guide later variety; no new species art was installed in this pass.

## River and levee

Preston's Screenshot_160.png through Screenshot_167.png show the Elwood Gladden Levee and Roseport Landing by the Pony Express bridges, then the wider river bend and both towns. Preserve his orientation: Elwood is on the left/west, St. Joseph is on the right/east, and the Missouri River lies between them. Satellite and map views should be considered together.

The visible levee reads as a broad grass-covered earth embankment, a narrow track along its crest, sloping sides, and a wooded strip/floodplain between portions of it and the river. The existing short RetainingWallWest/East sprites are small masonry retaining walls beside the route path, not a representation of this levee. A later levee treatment should use raised ground and a crest path, following the river bend, rather than extending the small wall into a flood barrier.
